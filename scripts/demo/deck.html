📞 Live Call Guardian
Put a suspicious call on speaker. Gemini 3.8 Live flags scam tactics within about a second, shows a risk meter and speaks a warning in the victim's language before money moves.
🔍 Scam Check
Paste a message or drop a screenshot / voice note. A multi-agent Gemini pipeline returns an explainable verdict, web-verified evidence, the scam's stage and the right national hotline.
🏦 Intel Console
Every check becomes redacted, real-time intelligence: campaigns clustered with Firestore vector search, Gemini threat bulletins with bank controls, and an analyst agent.
Built on Gemini (3.8 Flash · 3.8 Live · Flash-Lite · embeddings · TTS) · Cloud Run · Firestore · Firebase Hosting / Auth / App Check · Terraform · EN / BM / 中文
How it's different
• Existing tools are reactive and indicator-based - they need a known bad number or link. Kawal is behaviour-based and real-time: it recognises manipulation tactics from a brand-new number.

• Multimodal and multilingual (calls, voice notes, screenshots, text; code-switching EN/BM/中文) and explainable - quoted evidence, kill-chain stage, sources.

• Closes the loop from citizen to bank.
How it solves the problem
• Interrupts the persuasion at the moment money is about to move: risk meter → full-screen alert → spoken warning → one-tap NSRC 997 / ScamShield 1799 / CICC 1326.

• Removes the doubt with a calm explanation grounded in official sources.

• Faster detection for banks: campaigns surface from citizen reports, with concrete controls (step-up auth during active calls, cooling-off for new payees).
USP
• Real-time, in-call, in-language intervention with Gemini Live + function calling - silent by default.

• Dual-path detection: sub-second Live flags + calibrated Flash-Lite re-scoring → 0 alerts on a genuine bank call.

• Privacy-first network effect: no raw content stored; HMAC fingerprints; TTL.

• Production-shaped: Terraform, App Check, near-zero-cost guard-rails.
